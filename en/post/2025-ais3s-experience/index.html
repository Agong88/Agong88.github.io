2025 AIS3's Experience

Aug 13, 2025
3 minute read
中文

Preface

I see most people are writing introductions and team selection stuff, so I guess I’ll write something too.

AIS3: Advanced Information Security - Summer School

It’s basically a camp where you quickly bond with your teammates over seven days and work together on a project. Of course, besides the project, there are also courses. You can see “Advanced” in the AIS3 name - as the name suggests, the classes are pretty hardcore and you get very little sleep ☠️

For team selection, I got in through the Pre-exam, so I had these options: (A) Software, Web & IoT Security
(B) Threat Intelligence & Defense
(C) Advanced Security Competition
(D) Cross-domain Information Security

I put Software, Web & IoT Security as my first choice and Cross-domain Information Security as my second choice, and I got my second choice.

Cross-domain Information Security - as the name suggests, the scope is huge, so you can basically do any project topic.

Day 0

This day was cool. Chatting with teammates I didn’t know yet, and turns out even though we’d never met, we had quite a few mutual friends. The tech circle is really small, I guess.

Of course, had to play some GTA that I hadn’t played in a while since Day 0 was when we could still have fun

Day 1

First thing in the morning, I saw the whole week’s schedule and took a pic.

Same old routine - officials giving speeches and photo time.

Then the first class and I was already dying. Windows Kernel that I’d never touched before - almost fell asleep but managed to hang in there.

Lunch was free Subway. Free food is always awesome.

The afternoon classes were more understandable.

The ice-breaker activities were pretty fun. We solved three problems.

Day 2

Most of Day 2 sounded okay, but I really couldn’t understand the quantum security stuff.

The all-you-can-eat fried chicken, pizza, and drinks in the evening was nice. Ate pretty well. My teammate even took a whole box of pizza back to the dorm to go with our project discussions.

Day 3

I remember we decided in the morning to do a Side Channel Attack related to keyboards, and we needed to get a physical keyboard. I immediately thought of lalashark in Hsinchu. Super grateful they could come to NCTU for a face-to-face keyboard exchange despite being so busy. We should definitely grab a meal sometime - it’s been forever since we chatted.

Day 4

First thing in the morning, I snuck out to meet at the grad school building for the keyboard exchange (thanks to the STAFF for letting me slip out for 10 minutes), and then the oloo bike broke down.

After getting it, we started recording our dataset. We recorded for a total of 6 hours - some in the noisy venue, and some in the dorm after lights out.

Day 5

I feel like I was pretty useless on Day 5. Spent the morning working on slides and speech notes, and basically just scrolled my phone and played games in the afternoon.

In the evening, we presented once to the TAs, made some small changes to the slides, and called it a day around 2 AM.

Day 6

Day 6 morning was basically either sleeping or preparing speech notes (which we didn’t end up using anyway).

Soon it was noon, and thinking we were too tired before the presentation, we decided we had to eat something good. So I ate lunch that cost twice my budget from the previous days. Then after that, thinking “the presentation’s over, let’s eat well,” I kept ordering expensive stuff and burned through money super fast 😢

I thought our presentation was just okay, but two judges seemed pretty interested in our work. Either we were definitely getting the best project award or we had nothing special, but I figured since it was my first time at AIS3, just getting the completion certificate would be good enough. So I went to eat at Costco, but couldn’t get in and ended up eating McDonald’s at the girls’ dorm instead.

In the evening, I originally planned to wash up and sleep early, but remembered I still had 100 minutes left on my 200-minute oloo rental, so I went out for a ride around NTHU and NTHU night market.

When I rode back, I still had 50 minutes left, so I went downstairs to see how hard everyone was grinding. Saw someone had already taken down Windows

Later around 1 AM, after eating a late-night snack and couldn’t sleep, I went out for another 30-minute oloo ride and saw this tragic scene.

But there were also some nice views, though I took terrible photos.

After getting back to the dorm, I went to chat with Panda and helped him rush through some documents.

Day 7

This morning was basically all sleeping, only got up to listen to a few presentations.

In the afternoon, just got up to receive awards and then headed back to prepare for summer tutoring the next day.

When I heard there were Jess clothes during the award ceremony, my eyes lit up 👀
Saw my number and immediately picked it.

Of course, had to take a group photo with the AIS3 sign (unfortunately forgot to take a solo pic QQ).

Project

Our project was called “Side-Channel Attack to Steal ATM Keyboard Input: Using MLP to Analyze Keyboard Keystroke Sounds.”

It mainly uses a microphone to record for MLP analysis of “the subtle sound waves generated when keyboards are pressed at different key positions.”

Summary

AIS3 is awesome!

Free accommodation plus a quality learning environment.

Plus they let everyone gather in the basement to grind together and pull all-nighters together.

This experience was really special.

Even though I didn’t understand all the courses completely, it was fun and I’ll definitely go again next year.
